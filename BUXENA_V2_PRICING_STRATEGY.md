**Rank rationale:** UKU 230 sits in the densest, best-evidenced band and carries the strongest margin; UKU 160 has the best freight economics and opens the same demand at a lower entry point; ELLA is our cheapest unit but lands in a deeply-discounted market, so it earns third place, not first.

### Do NOT launch yet

| Models | Why |
|---|---|
| **ILLI, ILLI II, ALLA, ALLA II, ELLA II** (BUH-02–06) | No supplier EXW. Market is now known ($5,547 / $7,290 medians) but margin cannot be computed |
| **All 16 EDA barrels** (BUH-20–35) | No EXW. Barrels are also the cheapest, most crowded segment ($4.6–5.3k medians) — the weakest margin opportunity in the range |
| **All 7 VIRU, NORD ×2, AURA** (BUH-37–44, 12, 13) | No EXW **and supplier not verified** — cannot be ordered reliably, let alone priced |
| **ITI Thermowood 2.3m** (BUH-36) | No EXW, no supplier, **and no verified capacity** — cannot be positioned at all |
| **EDA/VIRU 3.0–4.0m+** | Above the evidenced price ceiling; no comparable exists at that size in the research |

### What would most improve margin
1. **Confirm the duty rate and HS classification.** At 15% it is our largest single cost after the product — if the true rate is lower, margin moves several points immediately.
2. **Fill the container.** Freight at $150/unit assumes 24 units per 40HC; a partial first shipment multiplies it.
3. **Select and cost the actual heater.** The $750 allowance is a placeholder on every calculation above.
4. **Quote delivery separately** (already our approach) — including it would cut roughly 6–8 margin points.
