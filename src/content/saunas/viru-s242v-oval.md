The S242V is the four-person oval configuration with a separate changing room
and a 50 cm terrace, in a 4.1 x 2.4 m shell.

The steam room is 226.5 x 178 cm — about 7 m3 — with two upper benches, at
143.5 cm and 178 cm. The changing room is a genuine 173 x 178 cm room with its
own 178 cm seat, not a vestibule you edge through.

It is supplied unassembled and weighs about 1,332 kg built, standing on a
299.2 x 210.5 cm base, so assembly and a prepared foundation both need planning
before it arrives. Spruce or thermowood, 40 mm walls, flat-packed to
240 x 120 x 200 cm. Electric or wood-burning heater, specified with your quote
and not included.
