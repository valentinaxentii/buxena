The largest barrel in the range seats eight — a sauna for households that
bathe together, for guests, for the evening that turns into an occasion.
The steam room sits behind tempered glass; ahead of it, a genuine dressing
room behind a solid, lockable exterior door keeps towels and robes dry and
the sauna secure.

Even at this length the barrel geometry earns its keep: the curved wall
returns heat to the room, leaves no cold corners, and keeps the air volume
manageable for a properly sized heater. Thermowood construction —
heat-treated, dimensionally stable, weather-resistant without chemical
treatment — is built for year-round use.

Heater sizing matters most at this scale, and we confirm it with you before
ordering, along with glazing options and site placement.
