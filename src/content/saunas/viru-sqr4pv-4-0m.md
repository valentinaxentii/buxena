The supplier table lists the SQR4PV as a 200 cm steam room, a 60 cm changing
room and a 125 cm terrace — three zones inside a four-meter shell, for four
bathers.

The proportions are unusual: the terrace is more than twice the depth of the
changing room, so this is a model built around sitting outside. The changing
room is a vestibule for clothes and a door seal rather than a room in its own
right. The steam room itself matches the SQR3P at about 7.6 m3.

Spruce or thermowood, factory-assembled or flat-packed to 400 x 120 x 150 cm,
40 mm walls, about 800 kg on a 160 x 342 cm footprint — long enough that access
along the delivery route is worth checking early. Electric or wood-burning
heater, confirmed with your quote and not included.
