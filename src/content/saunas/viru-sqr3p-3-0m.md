The SQR3P separates a 200 cm steam room from an 80 cm changing room. Same
three-meter shell as the SQR3, divided differently: you trade two seats for
somewhere dry to leave clothes, towels and boots.

That 80 cm is a vestibule rather than a room you linger in — enough to close the
outer door behind you before opening the steam-room door, which is what keeps
the heat in on a cold evening. The steam room holds about 7.6 m3 with a
200 x 49 cm bench.

Spruce or thermowood, assembled or flat-packed to 300 x 120 x 150 cm, 40 mm
walls, 40 cm canopy, roughly 740 kg. Electric or wood-burning heater, specified
with your quote and not included.
