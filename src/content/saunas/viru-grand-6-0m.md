The Grand is the barrel for the property where the sauna is an event: a
21-foot form holding three rooms in a row. Guests arrive under a covered
terrace, undress in a genuine changing room — nearly two meters of it —
and settle into a steam room that seats up to ten without ceremony.

At this scale the barrel geometry still pays its way: the curved wall
returns heat to the room, and the 45 mm wall construction holds
temperature against a full winter. A half or full panoramic rear window
turns the far wall into the view — the option worth taking if the sauna
faces water or woods.

An electric heater with stones is included as standard; a wood-burning
stove with chimney is available instead. Timber, glazing and layout are
confirmed with you before ordering.
