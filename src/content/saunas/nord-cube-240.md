The NORD Cube 240 takes the flagship cube and gives it width: 241 cm
across, seating five without anyone folding themselves in. The full-glass
front — smoked, or the mirror-look reflective tint that disappears into
the landscape by day — turns the wall into the view.

The construction is the same uncompromising recipe as the Cube 200:
fire-charred exterior cladding, knot-free thermo aspen inside, insulated
panels that hold their heat through a hard winter, and a galvanised steel
base that levels on adjustable feet without a poured foundation. Floating
benches carry LED lighting below; the starlight ceiling waits overhead.

Glass tint, door orientation, heater control and audio are configured
with you before ordering.
