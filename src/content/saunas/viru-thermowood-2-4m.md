The VIRU 2.4m pairs a full-size steam room for four with a covered terrace
bench at the entrance — a place to cool down, towel off, or sit out between
rounds without stepping into the weather. It is the configuration European
buyers choose most often in this size class, and the one we recommend first
for American gardens.

The barrel form heats quickly: less air volume than a cabin of the same
footprint, no cold corners, and a curved wall that returns heat to the room.
Built in thermowood — heat-treated for dimensional stability and weather
resistance, season after season, with no pressure-treating chemicals.

Heater choice, glazing options and site placement are confirmed with you
before ordering.
