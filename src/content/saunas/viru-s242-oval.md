The S242 expands the oval steam room to a six-person layout while keeping a full
changing room. Same 4.1 x 2.4 m shell as the S242V, divided differently: the
steam room grows to 226.5 x 224 cm and about 8.9 m3, and the terrace is what
gets given up to do it.

The changing room stays generous at 173 x 224 cm with a full-width 224 cm seat,
so a group can undress out of the weather. Upper benches run 224 cm and
143.5 cm.

Supplied unassembled, about 1,332 kg built, on a 299.2 x 210.5 cm base. Spruce
or thermowood, 40 mm walls, flat-packed to 240 x 120 x 200 cm. Electric or
wood-burning heater, confirmed with your quote and not included.
