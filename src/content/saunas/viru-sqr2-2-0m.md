The SQR2 is a compact four-person cube barrel with a 185 cm steam room — 200 cm
end to end, standing on a 160 x 170 cm footprint.

The cube profile earns its keep at this size. Where a round barrel loses outer
seat width to curvature, the flat walls here carry a full 185 x 49 cm bench, so
the fourth person is not left on the narrow end. Steam volume is about 7.03 m3.

Spruce or thermowood, factory-assembled or flat-packed to 200 x 120 x 150 cm,
40 mm walls and a 40 cm canopy. About 640 kg assembled. Electric or wood-burning
heater, sized with your quote and not included.
