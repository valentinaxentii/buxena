The Panorama is two rooms in one five-meter form. At the front, a sitting
room glazed with a panoramic window — the cool-down lounge, the view, the
place the evening actually happens. Behind it, a four-person steam room
behind tempered glass, with thermowood benches and backrests throughout.

This is the model for the property where the sauna is a destination, not
an appliance: it faces the lake, the tree line, the garden you planted for
looking at. The layout mirrors to suit the site — steam room on the left
or the right — and we confirm orientation, heater and glazing with you
before ordering.

Thermowood construction throughout: heat-treated for dimensional stability
and weather resistance, with no chemical treatment.
