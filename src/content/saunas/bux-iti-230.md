The shortest depth in Capra's ITI D4 barrel line, for gardens and side yards
where a longer cabin would not clear the space. The curved wall removes the
cold corners of a square room, so it comes up to temperature efficiently for
its size.

configuration are confirmed with you before ordering — Capra's catalogue
does not state a per-model capacity for this range.
