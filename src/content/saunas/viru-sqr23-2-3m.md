The SQR23 provides a 214 cm steam room inside the cube-barrel profile. At 230 cm
overall it is a step up from the SQR2 without moving into three-meter territory,
and the extra length goes entirely to the bench.

Four adults sit along a 214 x 49 cm bench in about 7.5 m3 of steam room. The
flat sides mean the shell can sit closer to a fence or wall than a round barrel
of the same capacity.

Spruce or thermowood, assembled or flat-packed to 230 x 120 x 150 cm, 40 mm
walls, 40 cm canopy, about 640 kg on a 160 x 202 cm footprint. Electric or
wood-burning heater, confirmed with your quote and not included.
