The SQR2V adds a 45 cm entrance terrace and outside seats to the four-person
cube barrel. The steam room is unchanged from the SQR2 at 185 cm and about
7.03 m3 — the extra 40 cm of shell length goes to the terrace.

Those outside seats are the reason to choose it over the plain SQR2: between
rounds you sit down outside the door instead of walking back to the house.

Spruce or thermowood, factory-assembled or flat-packed to 240 x 120 x 150 cm,
40 mm walls, about 670 kg on a 160 x 195 cm footprint. Electric or wood-burning
heater, sized with your quote and not included.
