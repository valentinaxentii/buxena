The SQR17 is the shortest cube-barrel configuration in the supplier catalogue,
with a full 220 x 155 cm steam room. Three people, 6.3 m3, on a 160 x 140 cm
footprint — the model for a small garden or a courtyard.

The bench is 63 cm deep and 77.6 cm high, deeper than the 49 cm benches used in
the longer cube models, and 219 cm long, so you can sit cross-legged or lie
along it rather than perch. That depth is what the cube geometry buys at short
lengths, where a round barrel would be mostly curve.

Spruce or thermowood, factory-assembled or flat-packed to 210 x 120 x 150 cm,
40 mm walls, 40 cm canopy, about 590 kg. Electric or wood-burning heater,
specified with your quote and not included.
