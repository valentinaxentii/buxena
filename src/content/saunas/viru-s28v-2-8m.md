The S28V combines a 210 cm steam room with a 58 cm entrance terrace and outside
seats. The terrace is short by design — a step to sit on between rounds rather
than a lounging deck.

Offered in two diameters: 200 cm gives about 6.0 m3 of steam room, 220 cm about
7.3 m3. Worth noting that this model carries no canopy; the 58 cm terrace
occupies the door end instead.

Spruce or thermowood, factory-assembled or flat-packed to 280 x 80 x 150 cm,
40 mm walls, roughly 730 kg on a 160 x 195 cm footprint. Electric or
wood-burning heater, confirmed with your quote and not included.
