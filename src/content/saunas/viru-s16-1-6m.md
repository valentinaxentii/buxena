The S16 is a compact steam-room-only barrel: 160 cm long, 200 cm across, holding
about 4.5 m3 for two to three people.

Note the heater. This model takes an electric heater only — not wood-burning —
which means no chimney, no flue clearances and no wood store to find room for.
On a small plot that is often the difference between a sauna that fits and one
that does not.

The bench runs 188 cm at 52.4 cm deep and 77.6 cm high. Spruce or thermowood,
factory-assembled or flat-packed to 200 x 120 x 150 cm, 40 mm walls, 40 cm
canopy, about 500 kg on a 160 x 126 cm footprint. Configuration, heater sizing
and delivery are confirmed before ordering; the heater is not included.
