The SQR3 dedicates its three-meter shell to a six-person steam room. There is
no changing room and no terrace — all 280 cm of interior length is bench, which
is what lets six adults sit without negotiating shoulder room.

The flat side walls of the cube barrel carry a straight 280 x 49 cm bench run
rather than the tapering seat of a round shell, across 10.64 m3 of steam room.
Wall thickness is 40 mm.

Spruce or thermowood, factory-assembled or flat-packed to 300 x 120 x 150 cm,
with a 40 cm canopy over the door. Around 710 kg once built, so the base wants
to be level and load-bearing before delivery day. Electric or wood-burning
heater, sized with your quote and not included.
