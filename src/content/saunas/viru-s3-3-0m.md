The S3 dedicates almost the full three-meter shell to a six-person steam room —
280 cm of the 300 cm length, with no changing room taking space from the
benches.

It is offered in two diameters. At 200 cm the steam room holds about 8.8 m3; at
220 cm it holds about 10.64 m3 and the shell is correspondingly wider. The
choice is largely about how much garden you want to give it: 300 x 200 cm or
300 x 220 cm assembled, on a 160 x 269 cm base.

Spruce or thermowood, factory-assembled or flat-packed to 300 x 80 x 150 cm,
40 mm walls, 40 cm canopy, about 650 kg. Electric or wood-burning heater,
specified with your quote and not included.
