The VIRU 3.0m divides its length into two rooms: a four-person steam room
behind tempered glass, and a genuine dressing room behind a solid, lockable
exterior door. Towels, robes and firewood stay dry; the sauna itself stays
private and secure — worth having anywhere the sauna sits away from the
house.

The barrel form heats quickly and evenly, and the thermowood shell is
heat-treated for stability and weather resistance without chemical
treatment. For year-round use in a real winter, the enclosed entry does the
work a porch cannot.

Heater choice, glazing options and site placement are confirmed with you
before ordering.
