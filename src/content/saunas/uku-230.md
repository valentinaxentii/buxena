The UKU 230 is the family size of the rounded-cube line — seven and
a half feet of sauna room seating four to six, with a proper two-tier
bench layout and enough floor to make the ritual social.

The full glass front opens the room to the garden; the half-moon glass
back wall option puts the landscape behind the top bench. Offered in
Nordic spruce and thermo brown or black editions. Specifications above
come directly from the manufacturer's US-standard product sheet; heater,
controls and options are confirmed with you before ordering.
