Stand the barrel upright and the footprint shrinks to a circle two meters
across — a city courtyard, a side return, a corner of the deck. The
Vertical is a full-temperature, two-person sauna for the spaces every other
model rules out.

Height is the trick: heat stratifies upward, so the seated position near
the top of the cylinder takes the best of the löyly while the entry stays
temperate. The thermowood shell is heat-treated for stability and weather
resistance, the same construction as every barrel in the VIRU series.

Heater choice and site placement are confirmed with you before ordering —
at this footprint, door swing and clearance planning matter, and we help
with both.
