The NORD Cube 200 is the sauna as architecture: a charred-timber cube with
one entire wall of tempered glass, facing whatever view you give it. The
Shou Sugi Ban exterior — wood preserved by fire — shrugs off UV and
weather, and reads matte black against snow, lawn or stone.

Inside, knot-free thermo aspen, floating two-tier benches lit from
beneath, and a fibre-optic starlight ceiling overhead. The insulated
panel construction (40 mm FF-PIR) means it heats fast and holds
temperature in a real winter, and the powder-coated steel base levels on
adjustable feet without a poured foundation.

Glass tint, door orientation, heater control and audio are configured
with you before ordering.
