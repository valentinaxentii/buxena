The UKU 160 takes the classic barrel idea and rounds it into something
softer and more sculptural — a rounded-cube form, sized for three
to four people without dominating a garden.

Choose the full glass front for an open, panoramic feel, or the
half-moon glass back wall to bring the landscape in behind the benches.
