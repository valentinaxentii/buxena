**Subject:** BUXENA — U.S. distribution enquiry

---

Dear Anton,

BUXENA is preparing its U.S. launch and planning a first 40HC container for
**autumn 2026**, shipping to the **Port of New York/New Jersey**.

**One question before anything else: is BUXENA permitted to market and sell
Baltresto products in the United States?** Please confirm what distribution
arrangements you already have in place there and whether the territory is open
to an additional dealer. We would rather establish this at the outset than
proceed on an assumption, and we will not plan models or volumes until we have
your answer.

If the U.S. is open to us, we would like the following.

**1 · Dealer price list**
Your current dealer EXW price list for the barrel range (S2, S2V, S3, S4P and
the wider Ø2.2m variants), the Round Cube range (SQR2, SQR3, SQR4PV), the
vertical SH, and the S5PL and S424 models, in both spruce and thermo. Please
state per model what the base price includes and excludes — specifically
heater, controls, stones, lighting and any chimney or flue.

**2 · Minimum order quantity** — per model, and for a first container.

**3 · Production lead time** — by model. And is production made to order against
a confirmed PO, or do you hold finished stock?

**4 · Logistics** — crate dimensions and gross weight per model, units per 40HC,
and whether models can be mixed in one container.

**5 · Warranty** — terms per product, and how a dealer handles a claim.

**6 · Image permission**
May we use your product photography and renders, in writing:

- on the **BUXENA website**
- on **BUXENA social media**
- in **organic marketing** — email, newsletters, brochures
- in **paid advertising** — Google, Meta, Pinterest and similar

and make ordinary presentational adjustments for those placements —
**cropping, resizing, colour correction**, and placing the BUXENA logo on the
image? We would not alter the product itself, and would only ever use an image
of a model to represent that same model. A dealer media pack or high-resolution
originals would be very welcome.

**7 · Resale-price conditions**
Do you impose any **minimum advertised price (MAP), recommended retail price
(MSRP) or other resale-price condition** on U.S. dealers, or require approval
before we publish a retail price?

With respect,

**Valentin Axentii**
BUXENA — Where Wellness Starts
info@buxena.com | buxena.com
