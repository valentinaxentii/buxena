Dear Anton,

BUXENA is preparing its U.S. launch and planning a first 40HC container order
for **fall 2026**, shipping to the **Port of New York/New Jersey, USA**.

**Before we go further, one question.** Please confirm what distribution
arrangements you already have in place for the United States, and whether the
territory is open to an additional dealer. We would rather establish this at the
outset than proceed on an assumption, and we will not plan model selection or
volumes until we have your answer.

If the U.S. is open to us, we would like the following.

**1 · Models you manufacture**
Please confirm exactly which models Baltresto manufactures and send your current
model list with the correct designation for each — we want to be certain we are
referring to your products accurately.

**2 · Dealer / distributor pricing**
Your current dealer EXW price list for the barrel range (S2, S2V, S3, S4P and
the wider Ø2.2m variants), the Round Cube range (SQR2, SQR3, SQR4PV), the
vertical SH, and the large S5PL and S424 models, in both spruce and thermo.

**3 · Minimum order quantity**
Your MOQ — per model, per order, and for a first container.

**4 · Dealer discount structure**
Terms for a first container, recurring container orders and annual volume.

**5 · Payment terms**
Deposit requirement, balance terms, accepted payment methods, and any terms
available to an established dealer.

**6 · Heater and options pricing**
Recommended heater per model with dealer pricing for heater, controls, stones
and lighting. Please confirm whether heaters you supply are **UL-listed for the
U.S. market**, or whether we should source certified heaters locally.

**7 · Packing and container loading**
Crated dimensions and gross weight per model; units of each model per 40HC;
mixed-load guidance, best combinations, and approximate total units in a mixed
40HC.

**8 · EXW scope**
Exactly what your EXW price includes — export packing, crating, loading, export
documentation — and any other origin charges.

**9 · Lead times**
Current production lead times by model.

**10 · Warranty**
Terms per product and how claims are handled through a dealer, including parts
and replacements.

**11 · Spare parts**
Availability and dealer pricing.

**12 · Marketing and technical materials**
Please confirm **in writing** whether BUXENA may use your product photography,
technical drawings, dimensional data, manuals and installation documents on our
U.S. website, in printed brochures, in **advertising**, and in customer
proposals.

With respect,

**Valentin Axentii**
BUXENA — Where Wellness Starts
info@buxena.com | buxena.com
