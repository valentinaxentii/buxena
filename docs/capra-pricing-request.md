Dear Andres,

BUXENA is preparing its U.S. launch and planning a first 40HC container order
for **fall 2026**, shipping to the **Port of New York/New Jersey, USA**. To
finalise pricing, our loading plan and our marketing, we need the following.
Wherever it is easier to send a document than to answer a question, please do —
a current price list and a spec sheet will answer several of these at once.

**1 · Models you manufacture**
Please confirm exactly which models Capra manufactures, and send your current
model list with the correct designation for each. We are working with the ELLA,
ILLI, ALLA, UKU and EDA lines.

**2 · Dealer / distributor price list — the most important item**
Your current dealer EXW price list covering **every model and every
configuration**, including timber options, as pricing appears to vary by both.

So that one list closes our whole gap, please make sure it covers:

| Line | What we need priced |
|---|---|
| Indoor | ELLA and ELLA II · ILLI and ILLI II · ALLA and ALLA II — every H-configuration you offer |
| Cube | UKU 160 and UKU 230, including the **half-moon** variant as its own line |
| EDA — Thermowood | 1.3m · 1.6m · 2.35m · 2.5m · 2.8m · 3.0m · 3.3m · 4.0m |
| EDA — Nordic Spruce | 1.6m · 2.0m · 2.35m · 2.5m · 2.8m · 3.0m · 3.3m · 4.0m |

If any of those sizes is not a size you make, please say so — knowing a length
does not exist is as useful to us as its price.

**3 · Minimum order quantity**
Your MOQ — per model, per order, and for a first container.

**4 · Dealer discount structure**
Terms for a first container, recurring container orders and annual volume.

**5 · Payment terms**
Deposit requirement, balance terms, accepted payment methods, and any terms
available to an established dealer.

**6 · Heater and options pricing**
Recommended heater per model with dealer pricing for heater, controls, stones,
lighting and heater guard where applicable. Please confirm whether heaters you
supply are **UL-listed for the U.S. market**, or whether we should source
certified heaters locally.

Please also state clearly, per model, **what the base price includes and
excludes** — specifically whether the heater, controls, stones, lighting and
chimney/flue are in or out of the quoted figure.

**7 · Packing, weights and container loading**
Crated dimensions and gross weight for **every model**; units of each model per
40HC; whether models can be combined in a mixed container, the most efficient
combinations, and approximate total units in a mixed 40HC.

**8 · EXW scope**
Exactly what your EXW price includes — export packing, crating, loading at the
factory, export documentation — and any other origin charges we should expect.

**9 · Lead times and current availability**
Current production lead time by model or product family, and how you handle
availability: is production made to order against a confirmed PO, or do you
hold finished stock? If you hold stock, how do we check what is available at
the time of ordering, and how long is it held once reserved?

**10 · Warranty**
Warranty terms per product — length and what is covered — and how warranty
claims are handled through a dealer, including parts and replacements.

**11 · Spare parts**
Availability and dealer pricing for commonly required parts (glass, doors,
benches, hardware, heater components).

**12 · U.S. territory, and any pricing conditions**
Please confirm whether any U.S. territorial restrictions or existing exclusive
distribution arrangements apply to any of the models above.

Please also confirm whether you impose any **minimum advertised price (MAP),
recommended retail price (MSRP) or other resale-price condition** on dealers in
the United States, and whether you require prior approval before we publish a
retail price.

**13 · Image and marketing permissions**
Thank you for the written permission you have already given us to use the
shared-drive images on the BUXENA website. To plan our launch marketing we
would like to extend that, and would be grateful for written confirmation that
BUXENA may use your product photography, renders and technical drawings:

- on the **BUXENA website** *(already granted — restating for completeness)*
- on **BUXENA social media accounts**
- in **organic marketing** — email, newsletters, blog and printed brochures
- in **paid advertising** — Google, Meta, Pinterest and similar platforms
- in **customer proposals and quotes**

And that we may make ordinary **presentational adjustments** for those
placements: **cropping, resizing, and colour or exposure correction**, and
placing the BUXENA logo on the image. We would not alter the product itself, and
we would only ever use an image of a model to represent that same model.

If any of the above needs to be limited — particular images excluded, a credit
line required, or paid advertising treated differently from the rest — please
say so and we will follow it exactly.

**14 · Technical documents**
Where available, please send the files themselves: dimension drawings, floor
plans, installation manuals, electrical guides (kW, phase and recommended
breaker per model and heater), foundation and site-preparation requirements,
heater manuals and warranty documents.

**15 · Configurable options — the EDA barrel line**
Our website lets a customer specify their sauna before requesting a quote. For
the EDA line we currently show no options, because we will not list a choice we
cannot confirm. Please complete the table below for **each EDA model** — or tell
us the answer is identical across the line, which is just as useful:

| | What we need |
|---|---|
| Timber | Which sizes are available in Nordic spruce, and which in thermowood — is every length offered in both? |
| Supply format | Is each model offered as a flat-pack kit, factory-assembled, or both? |
| Heaters — electric | The specific electric heaters approved for each model, with the correct kW for that cabin volume |
| Heaters — wood | The specific wood-burning heaters approved for each model, and the flue/chimney kit required |
| Controllers | Which control units pair with each heater, and which are built-in versus separate |
| Glass and front | Options beyond the standard brown-tinted glass door — panoramic front, full-glass, clear or bronze glazing |
| Door | Hinge side, whether it is reversible, and whether it can be specified left or right |
| Roof | Options beyond bitumen shingle |
| Benches and layout | Bench material options, and any layout variants (e.g. with or without a changing room) |
| Included as standard | Exactly what the base price includes — benches, door, roof, hardware, stones, chimney, lighting |
| Optional upgrades | Everything orderable as an extra, with dealer pricing per item |
| Accessories | Your accessory range with dealer pricing |

If the full table is too much to send at once, the two answers that matter most
are: **which heaters are approved for which EDA model**, and **what the base
price includes**. Those two alone let us present the EDA line properly.

Once we have this we can confirm model selection, container composition and
order timing.
